Creative Partnership

Making Bitcoin's hardest tech visible.

For almost a year, we've been Maxy's creative partner: animating the cryptography behind the protocol, launching the company, and building the brand's face piece by piece.

Overview

Maxy Protocol is built on one uncompromising idea: Bitcoin never leaves mainnet. Cross-chain swaps secured by cryptographic proof instead of custodians — no wrapped assets, no middlemen, no trust required. It's one of the most technically demanding propositions in Web3, and that's exactly the problem: the better the engineering, the harder it is to show.

That's where we come in. As Maxy's creative partner, we translate what the protocol actually does — the code, the verification, the proofs — into animation that anyone can follow. What started with website animations grew into a partnership that now spans the entire brand.

The Challenge

This is the most technical brief in our portfolio. Maxy doesn't sell a lifestyle — it sells cryptographic certainty. Every animation has to be correct before it can be beautiful: how verification works, what the smart contracts actually execute, why Bitcoin staying on mainnet changes the risk model entirely. Get one detail wrong and the animation doesn't simplify the technology — it misrepresents it.

Most creative teams would need months of onboarding to even hold that conversation. We're a Web3-native agency; our team works inside smart contracts, verification flows, and protocol design every day. So the technical depth wasn't the obstacle — it was the fun part.

Animating The Code Itself

The core of the work: website animations that visualize the code Maxy runs. Built as lightweight JSON animations, they show the protocol doing what the docs describe — verification steps, swap flows, proof logic — rendered as motion that plays natively on the website, inside documentation, and across investor briefs and explanatory documents.

The same system serves three audiences at once. A first-time visitor sees an elegant product. A developer sees an accurate diagram. An investor sees a team that can explain exactly what they've built. One animation system, three jobs, zero hand-waving.

The Company Launch

When Maxy prepared its company launch, the partnership scaled with it: a batch of over thirty commercials, produced alongside logo videos and launch assets — one consistent visual language across every piece. The animation system we'd built for the website became the foundation of the brand's public face.

An Ongoing Partnership

The collaboration is still running hot. We continue to develop Maxy's creative as the protocol grows — with the goal of managing the brand's socials end to end and giving Maxy its full marketing face: one system from website to feed.

Why It Matters

Maxy is the purest example of why our agency exists. Designers who don't understand smart contracts make crypto work that looks beautiful and says nothing. We got to do the opposite: take real cryptography and make it legible — not just as marketing, but as a step toward a financial world where everyone can understand the technology their money runs on.

That's the work we want to be doing. And with Maxy, we get to do it every day.
